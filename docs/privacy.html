☀️ Skymoji Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Skymoji shows the weather as animated emoji scenes. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. This page explains what data the app uses and who else sees it.

Your location

If you allow it, Skymoji uses your location while the app is open to show the weather where you are. Your coordinates are sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the forecast and to Apple's geocoding service to get your city's name. We don't store or collect your location.

Your cities

The cities you add are saved on your device and synced to your other devices through your own iCloud account. We can't see them.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, such as an advertising identifier, IP address and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Skymoji+ removes all ads.

Purchases

Skymoji+ is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment, and the app only checks on your device whether you have an active purchase. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, track you across other companies' apps for our own purposes, or collect analytics about you.

Children

Skymoji isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
